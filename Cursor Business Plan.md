# Cursor Business Plan

## Strategic Account Executive

### Executive Summary
Cursor is an AI-native code editor designed to help developers and engineering teams ship software faster with high-quality outcomes. This business plan outlines how Cursor can continue scaling from individual developer adoption into durable enterprise expansion through a product-led and sales-assisted go-to-market model.

### Mission and Vision
- **Mission:** Automate coding and improve developer productivity.
- **Vision:** Become the default AI development environment for modern software teams.

### Problem
Software teams face growing delivery pressure, rising codebase complexity, and inconsistent AI tooling experiences. Existing workflows often force context switching across IDEs, copilots, chat tools, and manual review cycles.

### Solution
Cursor unifies coding, AI assistance, and agentic workflows inside a single AI-native development environment that supports:
- Context-aware code generation and editing
- Multi-file reasoning and refactoring
- Team-level controls for security, governance, and compliance
- Scalable usage across individual developers and enterprise organizations

### Market Opportunity
- Large and growing global developer population
- Strong enterprise demand for AI productivity tools
- Expanding budgets for secure, governed developer platforms
- Continued transition from AI add-ons to AI-native workflows

### Business Model
Cursor monetizes through a multi-tier subscription strategy:
- **Individual plans** for hobbyists and professional developers
- **Team plans** for collaboration, reporting, and centralized controls
- **Enterprise plans** for advanced identity, governance, and support requirements

### Product Strategy
1. **AI-native editor advantage:** Deep integration over extension-based experiences
2. **Speed and reliability:** Fast, useful responses in real coding workflows
3. **Enterprise readiness:** Admin controls, auditability, and policy features
4. **Continuous innovation:** Rapid model and workflow upgrades without disrupting core UX

### Go-to-Market Strategy
1. **Product-led growth:** Drive self-serve adoption among developers
2. **Sales-assisted conversion:** Expand high-usage teams into paid organizational plans
3. **Enterprise expansion:** Land-and-expand within regulated and large-scale environments
4. **Partner ecosystem:** Strengthen technical and commercial partnerships for distribution

### Competitive Differentiation
- Full AI-native IDE experience rather than AI as a bolt-on
- Strong user experience focus for day-to-day coding workflows
- Fast product iteration cadence
- High trust posture for business and enterprise adoption

### Financial Plan (High-Level)
- **Revenue growth:** Increase paid conversion, ARPU, and enterprise contract value
- **Margin expansion:** Improve model routing efficiency and infrastructure utilization
- **Retention focus:** Strengthen daily active usage and cross-team expansion
- **Investment priorities:** Product R&D, enterprise GTM coverage, and customer success

### Operating Plan
- Expand engineering and research capacity for core product differentiation
- Build repeatable enterprise sales motions and implementation playbooks
- Deepen onboarding and enablement for faster time-to-value
- Maintain high reliability, security posture, and customer support quality

### Key Risks and Mitigations
- **Model cost volatility:** Mitigate through routing optimization and pricing strategy
- **Platform competition:** Differentiate through superior integrated UX and velocity
- **Enterprise buying cycles:** Offset with PLG funnel strength and proof-of-value programs
- **Security expectations:** Invest in governance controls, transparency, and compliance readiness

### Success Metrics
- New user activation and weekly active usage
- Paid conversion across individual and team plans
- Net revenue retention and enterprise expansion rate
- Developer productivity outcomes and satisfaction (NPS/CSAT)

### Conclusion
Cursor is positioned to become core infrastructure for AI-assisted software development. By combining product-led adoption with enterprise-grade capabilities, Cursor can sustain rapid growth while building long-term defensibility.
